## Style Prompt

LeadScope product cinema: dark evidence-room backdrop, signal cyan as the primary highlight, warm evidence green for confirmations, restrained caution amber for operator risk. Typography should feel analytical and premium, with bold wide headlines and mono supporting stats. Motion should feel deliberate and governed rather than flashy.

## Colors

- `#08111B` background canvas
- `#0E1D2D` panel surface
- `#74E1F7` signal cyan
- `#7BE0AE` evidence green
- `#F3C86B` caution amber

## Typography

- `Geist` for headline and body copy
- `Geist Mono` for metrics, timestamps, and evidence labels

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic purple AI gradients.
- Do not use glassmorphism-heavy cards.
- Do not animate layout properties when transforms work.
- Do not make scenes feel playful; keep them operator-grade.
